title: The web app

The web app at [cards.timmo.dev](https://cards.timmo.dev) opens cards and
makes them. Install it from the browser to keep it on your phone or computer.

## Opening a card

A card's link opens the viewer. Tap the envelope to take the card out, tap
again to open it, then drag to turn it. Sound starts on the first tap, and
the mute button remembers your choice on that device. The page around the
card follows light or dark mode, while the card keeps its own design.

**Edit a copy** at the top opens the card in the editor as a new card of your
own, filled in from it, with its pictures, video and music. Saving keeps the
copy on your device, and the one you were sent stays as it is.

## Your cards

The app has:

- **Cards:** the cards you've made on this device. They stay in this
  browser and are sent nowhere; each one goes out only as its link.
- **Browse:** ready-made cards grouped by category, with a filters sidebar
  to narrow them by category, occasion, tone (funny, cheeky, punny, dry,
  heartfelt or sweet) and design. The filters stay as you left them.
  **Shuffle** starts a new card picked at random.
- **Card:** a live preview beside the occasion and four panels, one open at a
  time and all closed when the page opens: **Design** (design, colours,
  effect, size and how it opens), **Front** (its title and pictures),
  **Inside** (who it's to, the message, who it's from and pictures) and
  **Background and music** (the background behind the card, music and Links
  on the back). **Shuffle** under the occasion picks a new design, colourway,
  effect and words that suit it, in any tone or the one you pick (funny,
  cheeky, punny, dry, heartfelt or sweet), keeping who it's to and from.
- **Pictures:** **Add a picture** on the Front or Inside panel takes the
  public web address of a picture or video, and the card loads it from
  there. A card can have as many as you like, laid one over another: the top
  of each list lies over the rest. Each picture's menu sets how it sits:
  **Full** fills its page, **In a box** frames it (boxes on one page share the
  room) and **Behind** washes it pale under the design or the message. Inside,
  the menu also picks the left page or the right page, with the message.
  **Bring forward**, **Send back**, **Change the address** and **Remove** are
  in the same menu. A page plays one video at a time. The background can be
  its own address or one of the card's pictures, and the music is a track by
  its address. Under the front's pictures, a link opens
  [Where to find pictures](/pictures). **Links on the back**, off unless you
  turn it on, lists the web addresses the card's files load from, small on
  its back. **Save for a chat** under the preview makes a picture of the
  front (PNG), or a video (MP4) or GIF of the card opening, and shares it on a
  phone or downloads it elsewhere.
- **Link:** **Card in a link** seals the whole card into its link, stored
  nowhere, to copy and send; see [Links and privacy](/links-and-privacy).
- **Settings:** behind the gear in the app bar, beside Auto, Light and Dark:
  whether cards play sound on that device.

There's nothing to sign in to: everything the app keeps stays in your
browser.

## Saving for chats

Each card can be saved as a still of its front (PNG), an MP4 or a GIF, to
send where a link won't do.
