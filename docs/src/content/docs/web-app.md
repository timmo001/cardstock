The web app at [cards.timmo.dev](https://cards.timmo.dev) opens cards and
makes them. Install it from the browser to keep it on your phone or computer.

## Opening a card

A card's link opens the viewer. Tap the envelope to take the card out, tap
again to open it, then drag to turn it. Sound starts on the first tap, and
the mute button remembers your choice on that device. The page around the
card follows light or dark mode, while the card keeps its own design.

**Edit a copy** at the top opens the card in the editor as a new card of your
own, filled in from it, with its pictures, video and music. Saving keeps the
copy on your device, and the one you were sent stays as it is.

## Your cards

The app has:

- **Cards:** the cards you've made on this device. They stay in this
  browser and are sent nowhere; each one goes out only as its link.
- **Browse:** ready-made cards grouped by category, with a filters sidebar
  to narrow them by category, occasion, tone (funny, cheeky, punny, dry,
  heartfelt or sweet) and design. The filters stay as you left them.
  **Shuffle** starts a new card picked at random.
- **Card:** a live preview with the words, picture, design, effect and music.
  **Shuffle** under the occasion picks a new design, colourway, effect and
  words that suit it, in any tone or the one you pick (funny, cheeky, punny,
  dry, heartfelt or sweet), keeping who it's to and from.
  Pictures, video and music go on with **From a link**: paste the public web
  address of one, and the card loads it from there. Under them, a link opens
  [Where to find pictures](/pictures). **Links on the back**, off unless you
  turn it on, lists the web addresses the card's files load from, small on its
  back. **Save for a chat** under the preview makes a picture of the front
  (PNG), or a video (MP4) or GIF of the card opening, and shares it on a phone
  or downloads it elsewhere.
- **Link:** **Card in a link** seals the whole card into its link, stored
  nowhere, to copy and send; see [Links and privacy](/links-and-privacy).
- **Settings:** behind the gear in the app bar, beside Auto, Light and Dark:
  whether cards play sound on that device.

There's nothing to sign in to: everything the app keeps stays in your
browser.

## Saving for chats

Each card can be saved as a still of its front (PNG), an MP4 or a GIF, to
send where a link won't do.
